Before Hyderabad begins 🌙

So you're actually leaving now, huh, madam? It feels a little strange knowing that soon you'll be busy with hostel life and all the new things waiting for you, and your phone won't be around like it usually is. I just wanted to leave you something you can come back to whenever you get the chance.

Not a goodbye that makes you sad. More like a tiny time capsule of good wishes, silly thoughts, and a reminder that someone out here genuinely wants to see you do well. 🌷🤎 And before you ask: no guilt allowed in this little corner, madam. Only warmth.

A letter, just for you 💌

I put the long letter behind this little door. Open it whenever you feel like reading something from me. And no, madam, there is no homework at the end. 😭

Helloooo madammmm 😭🌷

So, first of all, Hyderabad??? You're actually going to Hyderabad and starting this whole new chapter and I'm sitting here like, wow, this is happening for real. It feels weird to think that you'll be away, busy with hostel life, classes, new people, new routines and all that stuff, and I won't be able to just randomly bother you whenever. Not that I don't bother you enough already. Anyway, that's a separate case and we shall not investigate it today.

I wanted to make you something before you leave. Something you could keep, something you could open whenever you get your phone back, and something that would make you smile even if it's just for a little bit. I didn't want to make this some dramatic goodbye scene because you're not disappearing from the planet, madam. You're just going somewhere new, and honestly, I hope that somewhere gives you so many beautiful things.

I hope Hyderabad is kind to you. I hope you find little places that become your favourites without you even noticing. A spot where the evening breeze feels nice, a food place that you keep going back to, a window with a pretty view, a friend who makes you laugh so hard that your stomach hurts, or some random ordinary moment that becomes a memory you'll tell me about later. I hope you get to experience things that make you think, "I'm really glad I came here."

I hope you meet people who are good to you. People who let you be your weird, goofy, dramatic self without making you feel like you need to change anything. People who listen when you talk, who notice when you're quiet, who celebrate your little wins, and who don't make you feel like you have to earn kindness. And I hope you remember that you deserve kindness even on the days when you feel like you've done nothing special.

And please, madam, take care of yourself. Properly. Eat your food, drink water, rest whenever you can, and don't treat yourself like some machine that has to keep running no matter what. I know life gets busy and sometimes everything piles up at once, but you are a person before you're a student, before you're anyone's daughter, before you're anything you feel you have to prove. You deserve rest too. You deserve to have days where all you do is breathe, exist, and get through the day. That counts as something, okay?

There might be days when everything feels exciting, and there might be days when you miss home or feel tired of everything or wonder why things have to be so difficult. New places take time. New routines take time. Getting comfortable takes time. You don't have to love every single day for this chapter to be good for you. You don't have to figure out your entire life in the first week. Just take things as they come, one day at a time. Let yourself learn. Let yourself make mistakes. Let yourself have fun. Let yourself be a beginner at things.

And if you ever mess something up, please don't turn one bad day into a whole story about how you're not capable. You're human, madam. Humans get confused, make mistakes, feel things too deeply sometimes, and need a little time to understand what they're doing. It doesn't mean you're failing. Sometimes growing up is just learning how to be a little gentler with yourself while you figure things out.

Also, tiny reminder from your unofficial department of unsolicited advice: you do not have to make every day memorable. 😭 Some days can just be you waking up, getting through your classes, eating something decent, and going to sleep. Not every chapter needs a cinematic background score. Sometimes the quiet, ordinary days are doing important work too, even when you can't see it yet. 🌿

I hope you find a little routine that feels comforting. Maybe a favourite snack, a song you play while getting ready, a particular route you like walking, or a small evening ritual that makes the place feel less unfamiliar. Home isn't always one building or one city; sometimes it starts with the tiny things we repeat until they become ours. I hope Hyderabad slowly gives you a few of those little things. ☁️

And if you have a day when you feel homesick or overwhelmed, please don't judge the whole experience by that one day. Missing familiar things doesn't mean you're not enjoying the new ones. You can miss home and still be glad you're there. You can have a wonderful opportunity and still feel tired. Two things can be true at once, madam. Life is annoyingly complicated like that. 😭

And madam, there is something I really, really need you to understand, so please read this part slowly, okay? 🫂💜 None of this is meant to make you feel guilty. Not this page, not the letter, not the fact that I made you something before you leave. You do not owe me a reply, a promise, extra time, an explanation, or a particular kind of feeling. Please do not turn my care into another responsibility you have to carry. This is a gift, not a debt. I wanted to give you something kind to keep, not something heavy to worry about. I can be responsible for my own feelings; you do not have to manage them for me. 😭🌙

I know you very well, madam — or at least, I know you well enough to recognise that you sometimes carry guilt like it is your job. 😭💗 I know you can worry about disappointing people, about making the wrong choice, about someone feeling hurt, or about whether you are doing enough. I am not claiming I can read your mind; I just know enough about your heart to want you to be gentler with yourself. You do not deserve to feel guilty for everything, and you do not have to be. You do not need to apologise for every choice, explain every feeling, or make yourself smaller so nobody else has to sit with their own emotions. You are allowed to take up space, madam. You are allowed to breathe. 🌷

You are going after something you want, madam. Something that matters to you. And you should not feel guilty for that. You are allowed to choose a future, take an opportunity, leave what is familiar for a while, study, grow, make new memories, and find out what kind of life you want to build. Please do not punish yourself for wanting something and having the courage to go after it. Your dreams are not a betrayal of anybody. Your growth is not selfish. Being excited about your future does not mean you care any less about the people you leave behind. You can love people and still move forward. You can miss people and still enjoy where you are. You can care deeply and still choose yourself sometimes. 💛🌱

And please, if guilt ever starts whispering at you, don't immediately believe everything it says. Feelings can be loud without being right. Sometimes guilt shows up simply because we are doing something new, because we are scared someone might misunderstand us, or because we have spent too long thinking that keeping everyone happy is our responsibility. But madam, you are not the caretaker of every feeling in every room you enter. You can be considerate without carrying the whole world on your back. 🫶🏻

I want you to have the kind of days where you laugh without checking whether you are allowed to be happy, where you get excited about something without immediately thinking about who might miss you, and where you can say, 'I want this, and I'm going to try,' without putting yourself on trial for it. You are not doing something wrong by growing. You are not abandoning anyone by learning to stand on your own feet. And you do not have to make yourself miserable to prove that you care. Please remember that, okay? 💜

Also, this letter is not a secret request for anything. You don't have to read between the lines looking for a promise, a confession, or something you are supposed to answer. I'm saying exactly what I mean: I hope you are happy, I hope you are safe, and I hope you give yourself permission to go after the things you want. If this page makes you smile, lovely. If you read it once and then get busy living your life, that's lovely too. The point is for you to feel supported, not watched or weighed down. 🌙

And if someone misses you, that doesn't automatically mean you've done something wrong by going. People can miss each other and still want the best for each other. Someone can care about you and still let you go live your life. Both things can exist together. I can miss talking to you and still be happy that you're going after something you want. I can wish I could see you more often and still want you to be fully present in the life you're building. Those feelings don't cancel each other out, and none of them should become a burden you have to carry.

So if you ever catch yourself thinking, 'What if someone feels bad because of me?' or 'What if I'm being selfish by choosing this?' — please pause for a second, madam. Ask yourself whether you've actually done something wrong, or whether you're simply making a choice for your own life. There is a difference. You can be kind without being responsible for everybody's happiness. You can care about people without sacrificing every single thing you want. You can love people and still walk towards your own future. 🌱

I won't pretend I know every thought in your head, because nobody can know another person completely. But I do know enough about you to say this: you don't have to punish yourself for being human. You don't have to earn the right to be happy by making sure nobody is ever disappointed. You don't have to feel guilty for living a life that is yours. And you don't have to keep proving that you're a good person every time you make a decision. Let yourself breathe, madam. Let yourself go. Let yourself be happy about what you're working towards. You deserve that freedom too. 🥹🤍

And please remember, I'm rooting for you because I want you to get to do the things that matter to you — not because I expect you to come back with some perfect story or because I need you to make me feel better. You don't have to manage my emotions for me. I'll take responsibility for my own feelings, and you can take care of your own heart and your own future. That's how it should be. I want this little gift to feel like a warm cup of chai on a difficult evening, not another thing on your list of things to worry about. ☕🌼

I'm genuinely excited for all the things you might discover there. The things you'll learn, the people you'll meet, the stories you'll collect, the little ways you'll change without even noticing. I hope this chapter gives you confidence. I hope it surprises you in the best ways. I hope you find out that you're capable of more than you thought, while also learning that you don't have to be capable of everything all the time.

And obviously, I expect stories when you get access to your phone again. 😭 The interesting ones, the boring ones, the completely random ones that make absolutely no sense unless someone was there. Tell me about the food, the hostel, the people, the funny things, the irritating things, the tiny things that wouldn't matter to anyone else but somehow mattered to you. I want you to have a life full of things worth talking about. So go out there and collect your stories, madam. I shall be here, prepared for the entire documentary.

But seriously, I hope you never feel like you have to pretend to be okay all the time. If a day is hard, let it be a hard day. If you're happy, enjoy being happy without worrying about what comes next. If you're nervous, that's allowed too. You can be excited and scared at the same time. Being brave doesn't mean you never feel afraid; sometimes it just means you take the next step while your heart is still figuring everything out.

I hope you take pictures too — even of the most random things. The sky outside the hostel, a funny sign, a plate of food that looks suspiciously questionable, a pretty building, a blurry photo because you were laughing too much to hold the phone still. 📸 You don't have to turn everything into a perfect memory. Sometimes it's enough to keep a tiny piece of a day and look back at it later, thinking, 'Oh, that was actually nice.'

And please give yourself credit for the small brave things. Walking into a room where you don't know anyone, asking a question, trying something new, speaking up, or getting through a difficult day without giving up on yourself — those things count. You don't need a grand audience or a medal for every little victory. You can quietly be proud of yourself. I hope you learn to notice those victories instead of immediately moving the goalpost. 🌷

I also hope you keep doing the things that make you feel like yourself. Keep laughing at nonsense. Keep being curious. Keep having your little opinions about everything. Keep finding joy in tiny things. The world can be so serious sometimes that people forget how precious it is to be silly, to laugh over something stupid, to get excited about a small thing, or to find beauty in a completely ordinary day. Don't lose those parts of yourself just because life gets busy.

And please don't think this letter means you have to reply in some special way or feel bad if you're too busy to talk. You don't owe me a perfect response, madam. This is a gift, not an assignment. I just wanted you to have something warm waiting for you, something you can read when you feel like it, without pressure and without any expectations attached to it.

I know I can be dramatic. I know I turn simple feelings into paragraphs and then somehow turn those paragraphs into entire universes. What can I say? Words are my thing, and sometimes when I care about someone, I want to make something out of that care. Not because I expect anything back, but because I think people should be told that someone is wishing good things for them. The world already gives us enough reasons to doubt ourselves. If I can add even one small reminder that someone is cheering for you, then I'm happy to do that.

So go on, madam. Go live this chapter. Make friends, learn things, find your favourite corners of the city, try things you've never tried, and give yourself the chance to become someone new without feeling like you have to leave the old you behind. I hope there are mornings when you wake up excited for the day, evenings when you feel proud of yourself, and nights when you look back and realise that even the difficult parts taught you something worth keeping.

And whenever you get the chance to come back and tell me about it all, I'll be glad to listen. Until then, take care of that lovely brain of yours, don't skip meals, don't be too hard on yourself, and please remember that you are allowed to enjoy your life. You don't have to turn every moment into a test you need to pass.

And madam, one day, inshallah, I'll come and meet you. 🥹🌷 Not as some huge dramatic movie scene with background music and slow motion (although you know I would absolutely be capable of imagining that 😭), but just to see you, sit somewhere, hear your stories properly, and have one of those ordinary little moments that somehow become special later. Someday, when the time is right and life lets us arrange it, I hope we get that day. No pressure, no deadline, no grand promise that you have to hold me to — just a small hope I'm keeping with a smile. Inshallah, one day the distance will be a little less distance, and I'll get to say hello in person instead of through a screen. 🌙

Until then, please don't feel like you have to keep this letter or any message from me at the front of your mind. Go live your life, madam. Be present where you are. Make the most of the people and moments around you. This little page will wait quietly, like a bookmark tucked inside a book, ready whenever you feel like returning to it. 📖💜

And if some day you read this months later and laugh at how ridiculously long it is, honestly, fair. I started with the intention of writing a few lines and somehow built an entire emotional neighbourhood. This is what happens when you give a poet access to a keyboard and absolutely no supervision. 😭✍️ But underneath all the dramatic paragraphs, the emojis, and my tendency to explain one feeling in seventeen different ways, the message is simple: I want life to be good to you. I want you to feel safe, to feel proud of yourself, to find reasons to laugh, and to know that there are people out here quietly wishing you well.

Most importantly, madam, I am rooting for you. Not just for the big achievements or the things people clap for, but for all of it. Whatever you're working towards, whatever you're trying to understand, whatever little dream you're quietly carrying around, whatever direction you decide to take — I hope you get to give it a real chance. I hope you find your own pace and your own kind of happiness. I hope life opens doors you never expected and that you have the courage to walk through the ones that feel right for you.

And if you ever think you're alone, just look at the moon. Somewhere out here, there's one guy looking at that same moon, thinking it's you, and ranting to it about his day like an absolute idiot. 😭🌙 Maybe telling it some random story, maybe complaining about something, maybe just sitting quietly for a while. The moon won't answer, obviously, but it's a pretty good listener. And for that little moment, even with a whole city between us, we can still look up at the same sky.

So, yeah. That's my very long, extremely Fazil-coded way of saying: I hope good things find you, I hope you find good things too, and I hope you remember to be kind to yourself along the way.

All the best for this new chapter, madam. Go make it yours. 🌙💜

I'm rooting for you, always.
— Fazil ♡
